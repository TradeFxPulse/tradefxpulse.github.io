01 · WHAT WE COLLECT

When you sign in with Google, we get your name, email, and profile photo. That's the core of your account. Beyond that, we only store what you create yourself.

02 · HOW WE USE IT

To run your account — nothing more

  • Keep you signed in and your data synced
  • Save your watchlists and preferences

  • Understand usage in aggregate, to improve things
03 · GOOGLE SIGN-IN

We never touch your inbox

We only request your basic identity — name, email, photo. We never ask for access to Gmail, Drive, or anything else in your Google account.

04 · SHARING

We don't sell your data

Your data only touches the infrastructure that runs the app — Supabase for auth and storage, GitHub Pages for hosting. No marketers, no data brokers, no ad networks.

05 · STORAGE & SECURITY

Encrypted, access-controlled

Your data is encrypted in transit and stored on Supabase's infrastructure. If you delete your account, your data is removed within 30 days.

06 · YOUR RIGHTS

You're always in control

  • Request a copy of everything we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Revoke access anytime from your Google account
  • We don't use tracking or ad cookies — only what's needed to keep you signed in
